Why separate tools?

Sometimes you want a world; sometimes you just want the screen to be black. These pages keep those jobs separate so you can open exactly what you need without digging through a bright interface first.

No account, no ads

The browser tools are free to use and do not require an account. Sound and screen-wake features remain under your control, and the pure black pages work without starting audio.
