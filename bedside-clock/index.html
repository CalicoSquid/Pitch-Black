A Dim Bedside Clock & Sunrise Wake-Up

A clock that tries not to become the brightest thing in the room.

TQW's clock sits inside the same mostly black world as the weather and moon. The time stays intentionally dim, the date recedes when the interface sleeps, and the controls fade away after you stop interacting.

Bedside clock

Turn on Clock from the main control strip. Fullscreen removes the ordinary browser framing where your browser allows it, and double-tapping the world also toggles fullscreen. The clock shifts by tiny amounts over long intervals rather than holding one perfectly static OLED footprint.

Sunrise wake-up

Under More → Wake, you can choose a local wake-up time, a 10, 20 or 30 minute dawn, final on-page glow and a wake-sound level that is separate from nighttime ambience. The wake-up time is the moment the sunrise reaches the brightness you chose; dawn begins beforehand.

If the chosen time is too close for the full dawn duration, TQW shortens the ramp and shows the actual start time before you arm it. A preview runs quickly and does not arm an alarm.

At wake-up

The world warms from a very dim horizon toward muted rose and amber. Night sound is gently reduced without rewriting your saved mute or volume settings. An explicitly armed wake sound remains separate, so you can sleep with nighttime ambience muted and still choose an audible wake-up.

Dawn is visual-only until your selected wake time. At that time, natural birds and a restrained chime begin fading in. Snoozing keeps wake audio silent until the new alarm time. Both use the wake-sound level you chose and remain separate from nighttime ambience.

When morning arrives, Snooze 9 min softens the light and sound before bringing them back. Finish fades the temporary dawn away and leaves whatever world settings are current at that moment.

What a browser can and cannot do

  • TQW can request a screen wake lock where the browser supports it, but support and retention vary.
  • The page cannot set your device's hardware display brightness or system volume.
  • The sunrise can work only while the browser keeps the page alive; a closed, suspended or locked page cannot be promised as a reliable alarm clock.

For anything you absolutely must wake for, keep a device-level alarm as the reliable fallback. TQW's sunrise is a bedside browser feature, not a system alarm.
