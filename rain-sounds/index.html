Rain Sounds for Sleep — Black Screen

Play steady or heavier rain directly in your browser, lower the volume, then hide everything behind a pure black screen. No music, talking, ads or account.

Press Play rain to start. Browsers require a user gesture before audio can play.

Rain without a glowing video player

The audio plays from this page itself. Once you choose Black screen, the controls disappear while the rain keeps looping, leaving a pure black browser surface instead of a bright playback interface.

Steady rain or heavy rain?

Steady rain is the quieter, more even texture. Heavy rain has more weight and movement. Neither preset adds music or speech, and thunder is not mixed into this standalone player.

How to use rain sounds at night

  • Start quieter than you think you need and raise the volume only enough to mask distracting room noise.
  • Use Black screen when you want the browser open without lighting the room.
  • Use Keep screen on only if you specifically want the display to remain awake; browsers and operating systems can still release a wake lock.

Want the living rain scene?

The full This Quiet World experience adds rain visuals, optional storms and thunder, moonlight, weather changes and the rest of the nighttime world. The standalone player above stays deliberately simpler.
