Black Screen

A pure black screen. No video, no animation, no bright player UI. Make it fullscreen and let the display disappear into the room.

Tap or press any key while black to bring the controls back.

What is this black screen for?

Use it when you want the screen itself to stop competing for attention: beside the bed, on an OLED display, behind another light source, while cleaning a screen, or simply when a bright desktop is the last thing you want in a dark room.

Black screen for sleep

If this is going beside your bed, the sleep version adds an optional dim clock and links into This Quiet World’s rain and nighttime ambience without forcing any sound or animation on you.
